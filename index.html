TechAgro Publisher

EN: TechAgro Publisher is the internal desktop tool of the TechAgro channel. It publishes TechAgro's own short educational videos about agriculture (3D animation with narration) to the TechAgro TikTok account, at scheduled times, after the owner signs in with TikTok.

PT: O TechAgro Publisher é a ferramenta interna do canal TechAgro. Ela publica na conta do TikTok do canal os vídeos curtos e educativos sobre agro produzidos pelo próprio TechAgro, nos horários agendados, depois que o dono do canal faz login com o TikTok.

How it works / Como funciona

  1. The owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the TechAgro account.
  2. When a new video is ready, the tool uploads it with its caption using the Content Posting API.
  3. The video appears on the TechAgro TikTok account, labeled as AI-generated content.
