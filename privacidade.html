Privacy Policy – TechAgro Publisher

Last updated: November 16, 2026

TechAgro Publisher ("the app") is a private, internal tool used only by the owner of the TechAgro channel to publish the channel's own short agriculture videos to the channel's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

  • Basic TikTok profile information of the authorized account (open ID, display name, avatar), used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • An access token issued by TikTok after the account owner authorizes the app, used only to upload and publish videos to that same account.

How data is used and stored

Data is used exclusively to publish videos created by TechAgro. Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never sold, shared or transferred to third parties. No analytics, advertising or tracking is performed.

Data retention and deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security and permissions → Manage app permissions). Revoking access invalidates the token, and the locally stored token is deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy: produtos4voce@gmail.com

Política de Privacidade (português)

O TechAgro Publisher é uma ferramenta interna, usada apenas pelo dono do canal TechAgro para publicar os próprios vídeos curtos sobre agro na própria conta do TikTok. Acessa apenas o perfil básico da conta autorizada e o token de acesso, guardado somente no computador do dono, sem compartilhamento com terceiros. O acesso pode ser revogado a qualquer momento nas configurações do TikTok.
